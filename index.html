05 BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I start without coding experience?

Yes. The Python path begins with foundations. Placement helps identify what to practise.

How do I unlock the next level?

Complete the required lessons and tasks, then score at least 70% in the level assessment.

Does mentor feedback determine my result?

Required tests determine task completion. Mentor feedback supports learning and improvement.

When do I receive a certificate?

After completing the required learning, assessments and final project evaluation. Certificate issuance is not connected in the current frontend demo.
